ABOUT

I'm a Senior Automation Engineer based in Istanbul, Turkey, specializing in the design, programming, and commissioning of End-of-Line packaging machinery. As the sole automation engineer at Vervo for Advanced Packaging, I own the full technical lifecycle of every machine we build — from software architecture to live production handover.

My expertise centers on the Schneider Electric PacDrive 3 ecosystem — including LMC series controllers, MC12 MultiCarrier linear motor systems, and multi-axis servo coordination. I've commissioned over 100 machines for major FMCG manufacturers across 6 countries, including Ülker, Hayat Kimya, Tadım, and Indomie operations in Turkey, Germany, Saudi Arabia, Serbia, and Egypt.

"Beyond machine programming, I architect embedded software systems — including a proprietary Production Intelligence Platform that delivers MES/SCADA-grade OEE reporting and machine state tracking natively from the machine IPC, at zero external software cost."

I hold a B.Sc. in Electrical-Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Technology Malaysia (CGPA 3.94/4.0) and have co-authored two peer-reviewed publications in machine vision and AI with Springer and IOP Publishing.

PROJECT_03
EMBEDDED PRODUCTION INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM
OEE · State Tracking · Speed Historian · Zero Licensing Cost

A two-layer embedded platform (PacDrive 3 Structured Text logic + Exor Jmobile IPC reporting) deployed as standard across the entire Vervo machine portfolio. Delivers MES/SCADA-grade production visibility with zero external software or infrastructure dependency.

MODULE 01
OEE Reporting
Shift-configurable KPI tracking. Products, rejections, boxes, stops, homing events, stop time. On-demand & scheduled PDF export from HMI.
MODULE 02
State Tracker
Full state transition log (Auto/Stop/Reference/Fault/E-Stop). Time-in-state + production counters at each event. CSV export for remote diagnosis.
MODULE 03
Speed Historian
24-hour rolling production speed trend at 1-second resolution — 86,400 data points/day. Natively on Jmobile IPC. No Wonderware. No FactoryTalk.
Standard on All Vervo Machines
