About

I'm a Senior Automation Engineer based in Istanbul, Turkey, specializing in the design, programming and commissioning of End-of-Line packaging machinery. I now lead the software department at Vervo for Advanced Packaging, owning the full technical lifecycle of every machine we build — from software architecture to live production handover.

My expertise centers on the Schneider Electric PacDrive 3 ecosystem — LMC controllers, MC12 MultiCarrier linear motor systems and multi-axis servo coordination. I've commissioned over 100 machines for major FMCG manufacturers across 6 countries, including Ülker, Hayat Kimya, Tadım and Indomie, with machine speeds of up to 650 PPM.

"Beyond machine programming, I architect embedded software systems — including a Production Intelligence Platform that delivers MES/SCADA-grade OEE reporting, production records and preventive-maintenance data natively from the machine HMI, at zero external software cost."
"Automation & controls engineer specializing in industrial automation, PLC programming and high-speed packaging machinery. I build robust, efficient control systems that bridge the gap between complex hardware and intelligent software."

I hold a B.Sc. in Electrical-Mechatronics Engineering from Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (CGPA 3.94/4.0) and have co-authored two peer-reviewed publications in machine vision and AI with Springer and IOP Publishing.

PROJECT_04
HMI & PRODUCTION INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM
OEE · Diagnostics · Production Records · Preventive Maintenance

A two-layer embedded platform (PacDrive 3 Structured Text logic + Exor Jmobile IPC reporting) deployed as standard across the Vervo machine portfolio. Delivers MES/SCADA-grade production visibility with zero external software or infrastructure dependency.

MODULE 01
OEE Reporting
Shift-configurable KPI tracking: products, rejections, boxes, stops, homing events, stop time. On-demand and scheduled PDF export from the HMI.
MODULE 02
Production Records
Daily, hourly and shift production records with bar graphs directly on the machine HMI, alongside detailed diagnostics screens.
MODULE 03
State Tracker & Historian
Full state-transition log with CSV export for remote diagnosis, plus a 24-hour speed trend at 1-second resolution (86,400 points/day) — natively on the Jmobile IPC.
MODULE 04
Preventive Maintenance
Total distance travelled by each axis and conveyor, so maintenance is scheduled from real machine usage.
Standard on All Vervo Machines
